# Assessor notes: one business change, five architect questions

These are authored reasoning criteria for fictional cases, not official SAP-C03 answers, a calibrated exam rubric, AWS observations or independent learner feedback. Accept other solutions that preserve the declared business invariants and make their assumptions testable.

1. **Two writers:** Stop an immediate return. The two Regions cannot each invent a winning appointment for the same slot. Compare confirmed booking IDs, write authority and conflict rule; fence one writer before selection. A stale return that hides either customer receipt fails. A deliberate forward recovery, reconciliation or business-owned conflict process are alternatives; DNS reversal alone is insufficient. Price the overlap, replication, reconciliation and retained copies.
2. **Acquired staff:** Directory sign-in identifies a person under that directory's rules; it does not assign an AWS account role or authorize a booking record. Compare a scoped workforce assignment and application-level shop membership with a migration-only, time-bounded role where appropriate. Test one intended read and an adjacent-shop denial. This packet does not create an AWS Organization to mimic an enterprise case.
3. **AI helper:** Relevance is not permission. Restrict retrieval by current actor and document grant, resolve governing policy from owner-approved meaning/version/effective date, and let application code authorize the exact credit action under a scoped role and approval policy. An old instruction inside retrieved content remains data. Missing current policy, conflicting rules, unauthorized source or changed action parameters should block or abstain. Compare read-only assistant and action-capable assistant with separate cost, logging and external-effect obligations.
4. **Standby cost:** Include both-Region idle and active resources, database/replication and storage, data transfer, health checks, DNS, logs/metrics/traces, AI requests or idle managed capacity if selected, backups, test traffic, old/new overlap and teardown time. Pricing must match chosen Region and service terms. Compare the complete estimate with limits and a reserve chosen for the actual account, after scoped identity and eligibility checks. A budget alert is an observation, not a spending brake.
5. **Automatic recovery:** Stop before changing traffic. A green process response cannot establish confirmed-booking integrity; a stopped attempt feed removes a denominator. Check an independent accepted-booking ledger, candidate reads of old and new bookings, writer state, coverage and freshness of the evidence, surviving capacity, approval policy and customer-path observation. A runbook may automate read-only preparation and pause for an authorized owner; an approval click cannot make missing data correct.
6. **Wrong booking release:** A business probe should read a known booking and verify the selected data version, identity, authorization and expected appointment before promotion. A negative control should demonstrate that wrong-version data blocks promotion. If the new version has accepted writes, restoring old code or routing alone may hide those writes; reconcile data and external effects, then decide roll-forward, guarded rollback or a stop. Compare the cost and evidence of each path.

For the school-document transfer, retain the **question structure**, not the booking implementation. The authoritative object version, scan result, reviewer identity and current eligibility, owner read gate and withdrawal rule replace the appointment slot and writer-fencing details. A confirmed upload is not approval; a scanner pass is not a human review; an application 404 does not prove a direct object URL is denied or retained versions are erased. Price storage versions, scans, workflow waits, logs and cleanup before any selected live trial.

For the fictional standby worksheet, the listed subtotal is USD 6.75. A 25% allowance adds USD 1.6875, making the first whole-run estimate **USD 8.4375**. It passes the team's USD 9 per-run comparison and would leave USD 91.5625 from the invented USD 100 balance if it were the only eligible charge. The separate USD 3 estimate also passes its own per-run comparison, but both commitments total USD 11.4375 and leave **USD 88.5625**, below the chosen USD 90 reserve. The [exact-case checker](standby_cost.py) reports two true per-run comparisons, a false combined-reserve comparison and `live_run_authorized: false`. Two independent green checks against the starting balance would therefore be misleading. Raise the replica, backup and storage quote from USD 1.75 to USD 2.75: the revised subtotal is USD 7.75 and its 25% allowance is USD 1.9375, so the standby estimate becomes **USD 9.6875**. That changed run fails its own USD 9 limit; the two estimates total USD 12.6875 and leave USD 87.3125, also below reserve. The checker rejects a missing or negative meter. The [separate commitment exercise](../costs/credit-commitments/README.md) uses different invented amounts to demonstrate local two-process coordination; do not substitute its output for this calculation. Arithmetic alone lacks actual account balance, credit applicability, current rates, complete selected-resource inventory, effective role, stop and cleanup evidence. A missed meter or delayed charge could change the result.
