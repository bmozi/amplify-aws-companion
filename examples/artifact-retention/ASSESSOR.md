# Assessor reasoning: artifact retention

These are criteria for invented scenarios, not cloud observations. Reward supported decisions and declared unknowns, rather than either universal retention or aggressive deletion.

1. **Quiet image:** the declared recovery dependency argues against unreviewed deletion. The old test does not prove current safety, access or compatibility. Assign an owner to verify or replace the recovery path, and make retention conditional on a stated need and review point.
2. **Running process:** current execution and future retrieval are separate. Inspect the exact referenced digest, registry availability and effective pull path, and test a bounded replacement while preserving the necessary artifact. Do not promise that cached content will always exist or that deleting a registry image stops the worker.
3. **New problem:** investigate affected scope and consequence, restrict execution as justified, build/test the repair and preserve appropriately controlled evidence. A vulnerable artifact may be retained for investigation without being approved for recovery. Deleting it cannot fix copies that continue to run. No exploit or complete remediation can be inferred from the supplied facts.
4. **Accurate preview:** rule selection matched its technical specification, but violated the required retained set. Reconcile both specifications, inspect every affected identity and rerun the preview. Include references outside the currently active service. Do not award “keep the last two” without evidence that two covers the required environments and compatible recovery paths.
5. **Archive:** require documented and tested retrieval/startup behavior, permissions, availability, retention constraints and total expected costs. The ten-minute objective is a requirement, not measured performance. If the selected storage mode cannot support it, retain an available compatible recovery target or change the approved recovery design with explicit consequences.

For the ZIP transfer, preserve exact tested bytes, evidence trust, destination, configuration/backend compatibility, access, retention reason and retirement checks. Do not copy ECR selectors, priority or timing rules to a different service. Source alone may not reproduce an earlier build when inputs have changed. Record whether the answer is a prediction, a design decision or an observed test. No real deletion, cost measurement, live recovery or independent mastery is demonstrated here.
