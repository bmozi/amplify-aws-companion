# Classification decisions assessor notes

Use after the [reader packets](README.md). These are provisional authored criteria; no learner has been assessed here.

1. The supplied business scheme keeps the combined draft restricted while customer details remain. Public input does not authorize disclosure of another input. Encryption concerns protection of bytes, not approval of recipients. A useful answer inventories separate copies and identifies the owner, purpose, audience and enforcing controls. Do not infer a universal classification hierarchy for unrelated organizations.
2. The preview is narrower evidence than the downloaded artifact. Attachment and metadata contents still matter, and the summary may disclose identity indirectly. Require review of the exact release package and contextual disclosure; a removed name or an AI assurance does not establish anonymization. No particular redaction technology is mandated by these packets.
3. Only version 4 has the stated approval. Bind the selected publication artifact to its reviewed version or digest and reject a mismatch. The original and draft do not inherit the sanitized copy's public status. For already-distributed sensitive material, identify destinations, accessible copies, recipients and incident ownership; do not claim that relabeling or deleting one server copy recalls downloads. Preserve applicable evidence and obligations under the real incident procedure.
4. An internal label alone does not answer which employees may see which customers. Specify a legitimate reader and record scope, then an unrelated employee who must be denied. Handling, retention and a hold are separate decisions. Missing business rules should remain explicit rather than being invented to make the case pass.

Look for justified decisions that change with new facts, rather than a list of security products. Strong answers distinguish classification metadata, enforced access, content transformation and version-specific release approval. AWS discovery, tag-policy enforcement, actual redaction, customer authorization and independent review remain separate work. This exercise creates no Organization or resources.
