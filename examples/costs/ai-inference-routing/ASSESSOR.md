# Assessor: AI inference routing and cost

The fictional global candidate has the lowest token subtotal in the supplied case, but its `eu-west-1` destination fails the stated US-only processing rule. The eligible candidates are single-Region and geographic. Neither is a production recommendation without a chosen model, complete routing destinations, current rates, customer latency/quality evidence and a reviewed interpretation of the data rule. The script reports a token subtotal only; it omits surrounding service costs and actual AWS billing.

If the approved location set grows to include the fixture's third destination, the global candidate passes *this simplified location check*. That does not prove the real global profile is allowed: AWS may add supported destination Regions, and its current profile list and organizational policies need verification. A rule change also needs the data owner's approval; editing JSON does not grant it.

If the cache misses, move the would-be cache-read tokens to standard input and remove unearned cache reads. The token subtotal rises under the fixture's invented rates. Do not assume the same relationship for every current model; check the model's read/write prices, support, measured hit rate and response usage fields. The cache may save latency as well as money, but repeated content can also be a privacy or freshness concern. [AWS caching guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/prompt-caching.html).

For a real review, ask the learner to state the full customer promise: permitted processing locations; response deadline; minimum answer quality; sensitive-data handling; maximum and typical input/output tokens; concurrency and retry shape; permitted model and profile; observability and retention; and who may approve a more expensive or broader route. A lower subtotal cannot override one of those constraints. AWS documents [source-Region pricing and destination routing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/cross-region-inference.html). This lab provides no live price, legal opinion or deployed policy.
