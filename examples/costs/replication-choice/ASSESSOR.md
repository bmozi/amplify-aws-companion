# Assessor: cost-aware replication

1. **Photos:** Live S3 replication handles eligible new objects after the rule is configured; existing objects need a separately planned backfill such as S3 Batch Replication. S3 versioning, rule scope, permissions, encryption and destination behavior require review. The cost sheet includes destination storage and lifecycle, replication requests, inter-Region transfer, applicable KMS and optional RTC/metrics. Noncurrent versions can outlive the visible object. [AWS setup](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/replication-how-setup.html), [pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/s3/pricing/).
2. **Bookings:** A global table can keep Regional replicas for local application paths, but replicated-write units and storage in each replica have costs. AWS says it does not charge cross-Region data transfer for global-table replication. A backup/restore option may cost less for a slower recovery promise but is not a continuously readable replica. Measure the real RPO/RTO and whole application route. [AWS billing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazondynamodb/latest/developerguide/global-tables-billing.html).
3. **Concurrent edits:** MREC, the default mode, is eventually consistent across Regions and may reconcile conflicting edits by last writer wins. It cannot promise preservation of both incompatible updates. AWS also supports MRSC with different behavior and constraints; selecting it requires checking current service support, write latency, topology, transaction semantics and the business rule. A single writer with fencing or an application conflict workflow is another design, with an availability cost. [AWS global-table design](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazondynamodb/latest/developerguide/bp-global-table-design.html).
4. **Retention:** Replication creates destination storage, and versioned old copies may grow even when new uploads fall. Lifecycle, retention/legal requirements and restore speed must be decided together. Do not delete a required recovery point solely because a cost chart rises. Test expiry against a restore case. [S3 requirements](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/replication-requirements.html).
5. **Location restriction:** Do not copy restricted booking data across the boundary. Narrow the second-Region promise or seek an approved architecture that meets the contract. The separately permitted photo path does not imply booking continuity. A successful photo restore is no evidence of a working booking writer.

The exercise adds decision and meter coverage, not implemented replication, a current quote, a failover drill or learner validation. Reject answers that price only the initial transfer, treat a replica as an application, or claim that multi-Region copies automatically preserve conflicting updates.
