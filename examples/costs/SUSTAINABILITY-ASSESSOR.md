# Sustainability decisions: assessor reasoning

Authored criteria for fictional cases, not measured cloud or learner evidence.

1. Baseline 96 worker-hours; candidate 12; reduction 84/96 = 87.5%. Intensity changes from 96/2400 = 0.04 to 12/2400 = 0.005 worker-hours per accepted report. Same worker size and accepted work are stipulated, not measured. The percentage applies only to this provisioned-time proxy. There is no workload energy or carbon method, complete component boundary or measured environmental result to justify the advertised emissions reduction.
2. From 04:45 to 06:00 there are 75 minutes, less than the stipulated 90-minute recovery. Completion would be 06:15 if recovery begins immediately. The 05:00 capacity shutdown also conflicts with that recovery unless revised. Earlier detection, less work to redo, faster verified recovery or another tested capacity strategy could be evaluated; none is proven by naming it. Five-minute daytime corrections require an appropriate daytime processing path or an explicitly renegotiated business requirement. The current overnight-only design cannot be retained unchanged.
3. Original and grown stipulated intensity are both 0.005 worker-hours per accepted report, while total grows by 13 hours, approximately 108.3%. With only 4,000 accepted outputs the new intensity is 25/4000 = 0.00625, 25% worse than 0.005. Quality/deadline failure also requires investigation independently of the ratio. Busy CPU can represent productive work, retries or waste; its percentage does not settle business acceptance or environmental impact.
4. For the reproducible report, verify the input, reproducibility, ten-minute access/recovery tolerance, downstream consumers, required retention and authorized deletion before removal. For the sole recovery route, preserve the recovery requirement and establish a verified alternative before removing it. Access frequency alone does not establish uselessness. Neither a lower byte count nor the word cache grants permission to delete required information.
5. The proposed region is ineligible if outside the permitted country; a general environmental claim cannot override the stated contract. Within the permitted boundary, availability and latency remain unknown until checked. Include replication, storage and transfer in any resource comparison and use an appropriate environmental measurement method for a carbon claim. Revisit only when requirements permit the candidate and workload-specific evidence supports the full design. No country law or legal interpretation is asserted by this fictional contractual case.

A strong response names a concrete uncertainty and a check that could resolve it. It preserves cost limits and data boundaries, distinguishes measurements from assumptions, and explains what would cause the experiment to stop. No answer authorizes a cloud run or changes an account’s Free-plan terms.
