# Capacity review: reasoning and evidence limits

Original assessor guidance, October 18, 2026. Do not show this before the learner's first attempt. These criteria are provisional and have not been validated by independent learner trials.

1. **Overlap:** current/final demand is 16; overlap is 28, exceeding 20 by eight. With four unrelated units and at least ten serving units, at most six other units fit if all units share this quota. That does not prove a feasible rollout: minimum instance increments, readiness, draining and rollback may require more. An increase has approval/capacity dependencies; staging changes the availability and recovery tradeoff.
2. **Units:** floor(8 / 4) is two machines under this single constraint. Capacity, permissions, networking and supported configuration remain separate. A theoretical maximum is not a successful launch.
3. **Capacity:** preserve request/type/location/time and error. Investigate compatible capacity alternatives or a bounded retry without treating quota changes as capacity reservations. Review the alternative's dependencies and price before applying it. This is a fictional error prompt, not a live incident.
4. **Recovery:** intended peak is 30 units. The normal Region's 40 does not establish the recovery Region's applied value. If its applied value were 20, the plan would exceed it by ten. Check target values, usage, support and practical recovery behavior separately; do not report an actual quota deficiency from a default alone.
5. **Race:** combined additional demand is 12 against eight observed unused units. The observation is not a reservation. The local SQLite exercise accepts one six-unit request and refuses the second with a shared conditional update. A coordinator can serialize its participants' decisions, but does not govern untracked callers or guarantee provider capacity. Recheck the actual target and handle rejected or unknown operations safely.
6. **Different boundary:** inspect effective authority and exact denied action for the first case. Inspect payload definition, actual serialized size and supported handling for the second. Do not weaken access controls or silently truncate business data to bypass a rejection.

7. **Duration growth:** average estimates are 20 and 80. The latter exceeds the stipulated reserved ceiling of 30; spare unreserved account concurrency does not override that function setting. Bursts, duration tails, scaling behavior and request-rate limits need separate evidence. This estimate does not identify a precise count of throttled requests or prove the actual workload is steady.
8. **Preparation:** provisioned concurrency prepares environments for the selected target; it does not shorten a database query by itself. Separate initialization time, handler time and dependency time. Compare a query plan/index or reduced work only after measuring the cause and checking correctness. Do not claim that more prepared environments fix database saturation; they may let more calls reach it sooner.
9. **Target:** inspect the exact ARN/name and qualifier invoked, the alias routing and the provisioned allocation's readiness. Unqualified invocation uses `$LATEST`, not the prepared alias. Applicable limits can allow spillover to on-demand execution. Prepared environments incur charges while allocated and do not guarantee all-path latency or bound total application spending.
10. **Dependency:** the model leaves 50 − 10 = 40 connections for the function, not 80. Persistent idle connections, multiple connections per invocation and other uncounted clients are examples that invalidate the mapping; pooling or a proxy can change it too. A function ceiling can limit admitted work under the stated model but is not a universal database-connection controller. Require scoped measurements, admission/retry effects, a rollback condition and current cost estimates before a real change.

Accept alternatives when the learner states their conditions and preserves the business requirement. Separate a correct calculation from a complete operational proposal. Require explicit unknowns for real capacity, configuration support and current account state. No numeric result here is an AWS quota or a capacity measurement.
