# A09 fleet ingress: internal assessor notes

These explanations do not verify AWS behavior. Accept a different design if it preserves the business promise and demonstrates its boundaries.

1. The body label is untrusted. Bind a device's accepted ID to authenticated connection context and allowed topic, or use an equally enforceable mapping at a trusted ingestion boundary. A connection can be valid while the body lies. Reject or quarantine before maintenance acceptance.
2. AWS IoT's rule assumes a separate IAM role for the Kinesis write. A rule error action can record a forwarding failure; it does not validate a successfully forwarded business reading. Record both the transport failure and downstream validation rejection with ownership, bounded retention and repair. A stream write is not a dashboard acceptance receipt.
3. The fictional model offers 90/second, under its 100 aggregate ceiling, but `van-17` at 70 exceeds the 50 per-key assumption. The toy model admits 70/second and leaves 20/second upstream, or 2,400 over two minutes; it does not assume those records are lost. Under user-managed partitioning one key stays together, so spare capacity elsewhere may not help it. Spreading records can lose per-device order; choose it only after changing the ordering requirement or adding a separately justified sequence/reorder protocol. The local model does not predict real shard placement.
4. Original event time assigns the reading's window; later arrival changes what is known. Exact same device/event/content is a duplicate, changed content is a conflict requiring policy rather than overwrite. Publish provisional versus reconciled status and monitor missing expected observations separately. A stale or empty graph must not mean safe.
5. Independent consumers need their own progress and replay access within retention. Under the toy caps, 70/second are admitted and a 60/second consumer builds 1,200 records of backlog over two minutes. Another 2,400 are unresolved upstream; a stream replay cannot recover records never admitted. At 60 new admitted records and 60 completions per second, spare rate is zero: the 1,200 backlog cannot drain. At 30 new admitted records, spare rate is 30/second and modeled drain is 40 seconds. Real retry, variable payload and quota behavior require measurement.
6. A copied credential may produce authenticated messages. Revoke/rotate the affected identity, inspect connection and publish evidence, compare with independent physical or business evidence where possible, and mark unresolved readings. No schema or rate check proves physical truth.

The lab's invented limits are arithmetic teaching inputs. AWS IoT device policy, IoT-rule role, Kinesis partitioning, delivery/error receipt and business acceptance require separate configured tests. ## Reading-ledger transfer

The second local fixture starts `unknown` with no accepted readings. Five of six expected IDs leave `e5` missing; the observed 85°C value is still a warning. The sixth accepted late event fills the invented manifest, and event time keeps the observations in the earlier window. Replaying an identical device/event/content tuple adds no row; changing its temperature is a conflict and preserves the accepted row. A claimed van 18 identity supplied under trusted fixture context for van 17 is rejected before insertion. Van 18's own `e2` is separate from van 17's `e2`. The tests also check a half-open window boundary and that missing or unexpected IDs do not become a normal status.

An empty expected-ID manifest returns `unknown`, not a normal/quiet result. The fixture refuses future event times rather than testing real clock skew; a learner should propose an owned skew and quarantine policy before live ingestion.

Require learners to state that the trusted context, manifest and clock assumptions are supplied by the fixture. It cannot prove a real device identity, physical temperature, complete source history, stream admission, independent consumer replay or durable recovery. An expected-ID list can agree with stored rows while both omit a real reading. A warning from one observed high value is actionable in this fictional policy even when other readings are missing; the absence of a warning from incomplete data is unknown, not safe.

The first fixture returns rejection statuses to its immediate caller but stores no reject journal. Require a named investigation owner and a separate bounded quarantine/repair design; a clean accepted ledger is not evidence that rejects were resolved.
