# Scenario practice: authored reasoning guide

Read after the first attempt. These answers follow the fictional facts and are not an official AWS scoring key. No independent learners have been assessed with this set.

## Case 1

B is the relevant read-only comparison. A and D change behavior before establishing the proposed cause and are outside the requested observation. C infers cause from timing alone. B still needs comparable requests and trustworthy observations; it can distinguish explanations without proving the supplier's internal root cause.

In the changed case, a version-specific issue becomes more plausible, while the normal supplier timings weaken the shared-supplier explanation for those requests. Inspect the actual artifact change and the slow stage, then reproduce the behavior where authorized. Do not invent the faulty line or assume that the observation covers every request population. Review unit 28 and the staged incident exercise for the distinction between a leading hypothesis and a supported cause.

## Case 2

B and C test the two required populations directly. A confirms successful execution, not correct content. D can pass with substituted or incorrectly valued rows. E tests responsiveness, not business records. The stipulated complete ledger is an exercise assumption; real evidence requires establishing its authority and coverage.

In the changed case, database row agreement cannot determine whether a technician was sent, whether another send is needed or whether retrying would duplicate the assignment. Add stable business-operation identity and trusted external-effect reconciliation. The local selective-recovery drill intentionally excludes that provider. Review the recovery lesson and compensation exercise before proposing a real replay.

## Case 3

C meets the stated design-only objective. A violates the environment restriction; later removal does not retroactively authorize the change. B does not change the permissions or account-plan consequences of the operation. D confuses local reasoning with observed AWS enforcement.

The future sandbox case needs its own authorized account/Region and identities, exact policy and attachment scope, intended allowed and denied actions, recovery access, staged rollout/rollback, evidence retention and bounded cost/cleanup plan. An organization diagram is useful preparation but supplies none of those live results. Review unit 16 and the credit stewardship record; do not provision anything for this question.

## Provisional assessment

For each case, evaluate answer selection, requirement-based explanation and changed-case transfer separately. A correct letter with an incorrect reason is not a demonstrated explanation. Report assistance explicitly. These criteria have not been validated as an exam predictor or a measure of job readiness; do not publish a pass threshold for this small set.
