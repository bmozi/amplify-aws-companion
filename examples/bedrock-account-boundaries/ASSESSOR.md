# Assessor reasoning: Bedrock account boundaries

The answer is a trace of identity, processing, evidence and cost, not a preferred number of accounts.

1. `local-us-boundary` keeps the application and inference call in `shop-us`. `shared-eu-reviewed-paper-path` places the call in `ai-eu` after a stated role review. Both have named destinations, local content-log locations, a retention decision, an observation plan and bounded fictional costs. Neither case supplies the real role policies, model access, account settings, data classification or prices. Passing the checker is only readiness for human design review.
2. The European case lists `us-east-1` as one possible processing destination while approving only `eu-west-1` and `eu-central-1`. It must be held. A real decision needs the selected profile's current destination list from `GetInferenceProfile`, applicable Region policies, and the business's approved processing locations. A `eu-west-1` source alone does not prove EU-only processing.
3. The application may authenticate a shop employee yet still lack permission to assume a role in `ai-us`. Review the role's trust policy, the caller's permission to assume it, and the resulting role's Bedrock scope. The checker sees only a Boolean claim of that review; it cannot validate IAM.
4. The case names `security` as the destination of Bedrock invocation content logs even though the inference call is in `ai-us`. The documented invocation-log destination is in the same account and Region as the configuration. A separate export could move specifically approved evidence later, with its own privacy, access and retention decision. Do not silently centralize raw customer content.
5. The fictional balance is $45, the estimated run $8, and the reserve $40. The remainder is $37, below reserve, so hold. The same $8 estimate is below the fictional $12 run limit and $15 gross headroom. Those separate checks do not cancel the reserve failure. Real credit eligibility, remaining balance and service prices would need current account evidence.
6. Adding `us-west-2` to the `single_region` case produces `processing_outside_source_region`. A human must still check the actual inference profile, chosen model, endpoint, logging support and complete service meters. Codex can expose an omitted assumption; it cannot certify account configuration from this JSON.

The checker deliberately uses a small minimum meter list. A selected design may add caching, guardrails, embeddings, index/storage, transfer, retention, support or other charges. It does not compute a bill or prove that its estimates are sufficient. Use [AWS invocation logging](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/model-invocation-logging.html), [geographic routing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/geographic-cross-region-inference.html) and [IAM cross-account role guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/IAM/latest/UserGuide/access_policies-cross-account-resource-access.html) to revisit any changed service behavior.
