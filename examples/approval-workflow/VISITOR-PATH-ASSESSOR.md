# Assessor reasoning for the visitor path

Use these explanations after the fictional [visitor-path exercise](VISITOR-PATH.md); they do not verify live hosting.

The two matching reviews bind the proposed action. The provider's matching row and local reconciliation support a narrower statement: its simulated effect was recorded under the expected operation ID, digest, destination, artifact and receipt. The provider commit with a lost reply leaves local state unknown until that lookup. Neither fact changes the separate site pointer. The first loopback GET still returns revision 0; only the guarded site update makes the later GET return revision 1. The wrong destination gives 404, and changed content or a mismatched provider row must stop promotion.

A cached old page after local promotion requires an observation at the actual host and relevant client/cache route, including response bytes, freshness and any custom-domain path. The local SQLite pointer cannot invalidate a real CDN or browser cache. A 200 containing wrong prices or broken links would satisfy a transport-level observation while failing the business promise. Define expected content and a representative customer journey, then check what was served and what the application did. Do not substitute a publisher receipt for those observations.

Accept another safe design if it keeps approval, provider effect and customer-visible result separately observable and handles a lost reply without minting a new operation ID blindly. The local program does not establish authenticated reviewer authority, provider idempotency outside its fixture, live Amplify delivery, third-party effects or reader mastery.
