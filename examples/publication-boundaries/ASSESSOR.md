# Assessor notes: publication boundaries

These criteria assess reasoning about invented evidence. No learner trial or live AWS result is recorded. Accept alternative designs when they satisfy the owner's stated outcome and identify how they will be verified.

## Packet 1

The native switch covers only one route. The push-triggered workflow remains a release path, and disabling future triggers does not settle the active job. The learner should inspect that job's stage and supported cancellation behavior, decide whether it may finish, and verify the actual resulting version. Do not award a claim that cancellation necessarily reverses completed work. Pause the relevant workflow and inspect other routes within the declared scope; retain the last website and backend because availability is required. A later harmless push and job/history inspection can supply behavior evidence once an actual test is authorized. A proposed test is not an observed result.

The two other repositories have no route in the supplied inventory. That supports a bounded conclusion about this inventory, not universal absence of webhooks, workflows or credentials. Removing the entire app would solve a different problem and violate the availability request.

## Packet 2

The role name has no enforcement value. Temporary credentials limit session duration; their permissions may still be broad. The local helper guard does not govern an independent workflow. Require trust policy and actual caller/subject review, attached and inline permissions, applicable boundaries or other constraints, destination scoping and downstream execution authority. Passing a role alone is not proof that every privileged operation succeeds: the receiving service, deployment permissions and execution role's trust and policies matter. Nevertheless, the supplied broad path demands investigation before calling the design scoped.

A satisfactory plan verifies intended publication and denial against an unrelated target using the real role and relevant conditions, alongside policy review. Standalone simulation is useful but insufficient for the whole service path. A frontend upload job should not inherit backend/IAM privileges merely for convenience. Exact resource/action scoping must be checked against current service documentation before implementation. Do not demand that a reader solve missing trust facts by guessing.

## Packet 3 and transfer

Restart is a release decision: choose and review the source and configuration, account for accumulated backend changes, build/test the intended artifact, identify a single release controller, then connect the job and hosted behavior to that revision. Neither a subfolder selection nor an enabled switch proves event filtering or serving identity. A filter needs explicit behavior tests for included app changes, excluded lesson changes and shared configuration/dependency changes that can affect the app.

Retirement preserves source but changes availability and data-retention decisions. Require ownership and dependency inventory, explicit disposition of stored data, removal/read-back checks and later billing reconciliation. Pausing builds, revoking repository access and deleting resources are distinct controls. Do not promise immediate zero cost or infer remaining credits from a historical receipt.

The shared-repository transfer defeats a simplistic “select one repository” answer. The reader must revisit triggers and shared build inputs while retaining destination/identity boundaries. Flag as unresolved any answer that relies on a named role, one switch or a green build as whole-system proof. Record first attempt, revised reasoning and transfer separately; this is not a calibrated certification score.
