# Assessor notes: ECS rolling release

These practice cases are not a calibrated exam. Accept alternatives supported by the stated constraints. Require the learner to distinguish supplied fictional facts from proposed investigations.

1. Three times 75% is 2.25, rounded up to three; three times 125% is 3.75, rounded down to three. No initial start or healthy-stop slot exists under the assumptions. Minimum 100% / maximum 150% permits a fourth task; minimum 50% / maximum 100% permits stopping one while retaining two. These are examples to evaluate, not universal recommended settings. Require evidence that the retained capacity meets demand and the overlap can actually be supplied.
2. The limits are two healthy and three RUNNING/PENDING tasks. This permits a slot, not guaranteed placement or successful startup. Inspect the intended service/deployment, task counts/states, service events and task stop reasons. Then investigate the indicated capacity, resource/network allocation, image pull or execution path. Distinguish unavailable evidence from a diagnosed cause; broadening roles without a specific denial is not a repair.
3. Both configured health boundaries must pass before counting this candidate toward the minimum. Possible investigations include the target health reason, check path/port, security-group path and application response at that path. A passing local container check may exercise a different route. Do not solve the case by disabling a meaningful check without replacing its assurance.
4. HTTP success does not establish the promised amount. A safe fixture should compare known expected totals, include a nonqualifying case, identify the responding candidate and define missing/late evidence handling. The already-alarming input has a separate documented ECS monitoring consequence. Require checking actual alarm state and integration behavior; a synthetic test design is not delivered alarm or rollback evidence.
5. A process exit is not the client's completion receipt. Require the request timeline, returned result, expected stored object's completeness/integrity and the effective deregistration/termination settings. Longer work can require resumable upload, durable progress or a safe retry contract. Increasing timeouts alone does not establish bounded completion or prevent duplicate effects.
6. Without a completed predecessor, automatic rollback has no eligible deployment to use. A recorded predecessor also needs retrievable compatible artifacts, configuration and data assumptions. Retain and test those for a defined recovery period; inventory their costs. Reconcile invoices already written rather than claiming that changing serving code undoes them.

Transfer: four times 75% yields a floor of three; four times 125% yields a ceiling of five. The arithmetic permits an initial stop or start, but the stated business requirement rejects reducing useful workers below four. Investigate start-first replacement with enough actual capacity, measured healthy workload support and drain behavior. Require a bounded estimate for the extra task and supporting resources. No deterministic scheduler ordering or customer traffic share follows from these calculations.

A strong response connects each decision to evidence that could disprove it. An answer that merely repeats “use rollback” or computes percentages without assessing capacity, customer behavior and recovery prerequisites is incomplete. No learner has been assessed by these notes.
