# Professional reliability: assessor reasoning

These criteria evaluate fictional design decisions. They are not a measured availability result, Route 53 configuration, AWS quota inspection, service SLA or learner score.

1. After either AZ fails, the two-zone design has 400 assumed application requests/s against 600 demand; the database's 700 ceiling does not repair that shortfall. Three 400-capacity zones leave 800 after one loss and the 700 database becomes the stated ceiling, leaving 100 above peak on these simplified facts. Two 700-capacity zones leave 700 after one loss but may cost and scale differently. None of these sums includes skew, failed-target removal, retries, warm-up or another shared limit.
2. A quota that permits a request does not ensure compatible on-demand capacity, and a capacity placement does not grant a missing role permission. Desired count is not registered, healthy, ready or business-useful count. Inspect the exact selected quota and usage, launch event/error, placement and role, target health, dependency reachability and a representative accepted booking before increasing traffic. Do not invent a successful fallback instance type.
3. A durable acceptance response needs an atomic business record and work intent, stable idempotency key, readable status and bounded retry/dead-letter path. Photo work can wait only if the product promise permits it. If the queue cannot accept, the application must reject, retain an independently durable intent, or use another proven path; it cannot claim accepted photo work from an in-memory intention. A retry must reconcile the original booking identity rather than blindly create another. The local drill is not SQS execution.
4. A replica can copy a wrong value. Isolate a suitable historical point, replay only independently supported later confirmations with stable identifiers, and reconcile messages/charges before reopening the selected operation. Managed failover and endpoint health are narrower observations than correct customer state.
5. Latency routing answers latency, not standby readiness. Simple identifies one destination, weighted distributes DNS answers, geolocation follows location, and failover is the closest DNS candidate for an active/passive preference with a suitable health rule. None can establish 90-second-lag data is acceptable or half-capacity can serve peak. Correct data, writer authority, survivor capacity, health and cached-answer behavior must be demonstrated first.
6. Location-specific content may justify geolocation routing with a planned default, but it does not decide booking write authority. A client retaining an unreachable or stale address needs a bounded retry/error path; the operator needs route and customer-result observations. Failover and failback still require accepted-booking reconciliation.
7. The database's reported failover does not guarantee useful SQL transactions or explain a canceled write. If all ALB targets fail health checks, the documented fail-open behavior may continue sending traffic to them; choose a safe application overload response. Operator access must survive the intended fault. Close on a representative booking and its durable record, not on three green service statuses. Estimate duplicate capacity and recurring observability/drills before a cloud test.
8. The supplied next-week scenario requires 726 attempt units/s; the old one-zone survivor is limited to 700 and misses by 26. The three-zone candidate leaves 900 application attempt units/s after one loss, but the 750 data path gives only 24 of modeled room. Reducing data capacity to 700 misses by 26 again. A 60-per-week sequence is four observations, not a validated forecast; retries, latency, seasonality, placement, database failure and accepted-effect correctness need separate measurement. Do not count every retry as a new booking.
9. Regional quota headroom does not reserve compatible AZ placement or grant the image role. A desired count is not registered, warmed and able to book correctly. Stage the health check, scaling and data-copy changes with exact negative controls; inspect target replacement behavior and shared dependency failures before promotion. A 90-second-lag copy cannot satisfy an immediate confirmed-booking promise without a status/reconciliation design. Fence the former writer before transferring write authority. End with an authorized customer booking and durable record under the intended fault, plus rollback and full cost evidence.

Accept a different strategy when the learner states changed business requirements, complete path and evidence. Reject “more AZs,” “managed,” “DNS failover,” or “queue” as a standalone proof. Current [Route 53 routing policies](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/Route53/latest/DeveloperGuide/routing-policy.html), [SQS behavior](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSSimpleQueueService/latest/SQSDeveloperGuide/welcome.html) and [ALB target-health behavior](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/elasticloadbalancing/latest/application/target-group-health-checks.html) should be checked for the selected implementation. AWS operation, actual costs, independent technical review and learner transfer remain open.
