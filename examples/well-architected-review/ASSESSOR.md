## Two-stage customer clock

The [follow-through model](pickup_window.py) changes dispatch to five seconds and adds a separate 30-second pickup worker. All 460 jobs leave dispatch within 30 minutes of their original arrival; only 79 reach modeled ready state under FIFO pickup (A 67, B/C/D four each). Rotating pickup order yields 95 (A 35, B/C/D 20 each). The original arrival time remains the deadline origin, so neither stage resets the customer's clock. More speed at the first stage cannot remove a slower second-stage ceiling. The learner should identify a real shelf/stock confirmation as a missing step and an accepted-to-actually-ready observation as the needed measure. An answer that counts dispatch success as a pickup success is contradicted by the case. A rotation answer that ignores A's lost deadlines is partial. The fictional five-second pickup variant changes a work-time assumption; it is no AWS or library throughput measurement.
