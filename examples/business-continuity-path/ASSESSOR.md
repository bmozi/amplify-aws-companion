# Business continuity: assessor reasoning

These are criteria for fictional design answers, not a scored learner result or AWS recovery evidence. Accept different architectures when their stated failure, cost, authority and tested business outcome support the choice.

1. Three minutes after the office loses access, an HTTP health response proves only a reachable process. The demonstrated data at 09:52 is eight minutes older than the 10:00 confirmation and eleven minutes before the 10:03 failure. The age exceeds the five-minute loss window if booking 111 is unrecoverable. A separate trusted journal or demonstrated later copy may change the answer. DNS routing does not validate data or fence writers.
2. A completed copy is inaccessible to that role until the target key and recovery permissions are tested. The assessor should demand a destination-Region restore, actual decrypt, selected record and dependency validation, plus a fallback if access fails. Source-Region success misses the destination key and network path. Check the selected resource's cross-Region eligibility and recovery-point lifecycle in current documentation.
3. The sequential pilot-light estimate is 33 minutes (4 + 17 + 7 + 5), beyond the 30-minute target. The warm-standby estimate is 19 (4 + 3 + 7 + 5) if all other assumptions hold. Parallel steps count only when their dependencies and safe overlap are shown. Neither number proves data freshness. Price the running footprint, backup/copy, monitoring and periodic drill separately.
4. Green DNS and lag do not grant single-writer authority or prevent the last-seat race. Stop or fence one write path with a verified mechanism, identify accepted operations in both locations, reconcile conflicts against the booking invariant, then test the intended direction before failback. Switching DNS back can strand destination-only bookings.
5. Replication can faithfully carry the wrong value. Restore the clean historical point into an isolated location; replay legitimate later confirmations from trustworthy evidence using stable operation identities; reconcile messages, charges and customer commitments. A backup-job alarm detects missing copies, not necessarily a semantically wrong total; add business-level checks and an owner. Do not manufacture a missing journal entry.
6. The eight minutes is a resource restore duration in the supplied scenario. It does not include application access, workload capacity, routing, reconciliation or customer verification. A full-path drill must observe those steps and a representative accepted booking. Restore-test charges and lingering restored resources require a current service/Region cost and deletion review; an automatic cleanup promise is not an observed bill or cleanup receipt.

The local exercises verify only their own fixtures. No case establishes selected AWS replication consistency, actual failover timing, IAM, DNS cache behavior, costs, learner transfer or customer recovery. [AWS Backup cross-Region copy](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-backup/latest/devguide/cross-region-backup.html) and [restore testing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-backup/latest/devguide/restore-testing.html) must be checked again before implementation.
