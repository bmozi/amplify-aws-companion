# Business continuity: prove the whole path

This fictional assessment follows [Unit 07](../../reader/BOOK-LOCATORS.md#book-8f2f6dd7b63d58b7), [A16](../../curriculum/ARCHITECTURE-STUDIES.md#a16-recovery-across-regions) and [E05](../../curriculum/EXERCISES.md#e05). Answer before opening the [assessor reasoning](ASSESSOR.md). No AWS resource is created and no duration or cost here is measured.

Maya's booking system confirms an order only after its primary database accepts it. The business requires useful booking service within 30 minutes of a regional failure and a maximum five minutes of confirmed-work loss. A second Region has a small application footprint, an asynchronous database copy and scheduled historical backups. The operator's sign-in service, decryption key, DNS route, customer observer and last confirmed-write journal are separate dependencies. For every case, write a decision record with: failure boundary; RTO/RPO arithmetic; authoritative data and old-writer control; chosen recovery method; backup/copy/restore receipts; customer-level test; signal and responder; steady and rehearsal cost categories; stop condition; and what the exercise cannot prove.

1. **A fast route to old data.** At 10:00 the primary confirms booking 111. At 10:03 the source Region stops responding to the office. At 10:06 the destination application's health check succeeds. Its latest demonstrated booking is 110 at 09:52. A teammate proposes a Route 53 failover. Is routing the first safe action? What exactly is the observed age of the destination state, and what additional evidence would establish the business RPO?
2. **The copy that cannot be opened.** A scheduled cross-Region AWS Backup copy reports completion at 09:55. At 10:03 the source Region fails, but the destination key policy does not permit the recovery role to decrypt. Compare the copy-job result with a usable recovery point. Identify the access check, restore validation and owner needed before the plan can count this copy. Explain why a successful source-Region restore test would be insufficient.
3. **A pilot light under a clock.** The destination has data and networking, but no application workers. Detection and approval take 4 minutes, workers and dependencies take 17, data reconciliation takes 7, and customer-route verification takes 5. Compare the sequential total with the 30-minute target. What overlap could be real rather than assumed? Compare a warm standby that reduces preparation to 3 minutes, while adding recurring compute and monitoring charges.
4. **An old writer is still alive.** The source Region becomes reachable to one worker after the destination has begun accepting bookings. Both can write the last available appointment. The DNS health check and replica-lag metric are green. State the fencing and conflict-resolution evidence needed before either location is authoritative. What must be reconciled before failback?
5. **A replicated mistake.** A faulty update changes a booking total at 09:40; the wrong value appears in both Regions. The latest clean historical backup is from 09:30, while legitimate new bookings were confirmed between 09:30 and 10:00. Select a recovery point, name the independent journal/replay and external-effect checks, and explain why a fresh replica does not solve this incident. What monitoring should have detected backup failure or business-data corruption sooner?
6. **A passing restore job.** AWS Backup restore testing reports that a database resource was restored in 8 minutes. The application cannot connect because its security path is missing, and the target lacks capacity for the normal booking rate. Decide what the job establishes, what additional full-path test is needed, and whether the 8-minute result can be reported as the application RTO. List restore-test, duplicate-resource, data-transfer, log and cleanup cost questions for a future bounded run.

Use the existing [local recovery reconciliation](../recovery-reconciliation/README.md), [recovery gate](../recovery-automation/README.md), and [regional tabletop](../regional-recovery/README.md) for bounded behavior and changed cases. Have Codex challenge your decision by removing one receipt and by proposing a healthy-looking signal that hides lost confirmed work. Treat its output as a review prompt, not a measured recovery result.

An eventual authorized cloud rehearsal would first verify scoped non-root permissions, service and Region eligibility, current prices, a conservative whole-run estimate against account-chosen spending and reserve limits, and cleanup. This packet does not authorize a live multi-Region build or Organization-dependent work.
