Set a spending boundary before building

Distinguish estimates, alerts and technical controls, then prove what remains after cleanup.

Your outcome

Distinguish estimates, alerts and technical controls, then prove what remains after cleanup.

Suggested time: 45–60 minutes. Local worksheet first; notification setup requires your own budget and recipient.

A rented workshop still costs money after you leave

Closing a browser does not return AWS resources. A failed deployment can leave resources behind too. Before a cloud exercise, name the account, Region, payer, working budget, duration and cleanup owner.

Predict before you act

A teammate creates a five-dollar alert and starts an unlimited stress test. Can the bill exceed five dollars? Yes. Billing and alert delivery can lag usage; a threshold is not a universal spending cap.

Estimate → Limit workload → Observe → Remove → Reconcile bill

Do the local exercise

In the companion repository, read reader/COST-AND-CREDIT-FIELD-LESSON.md. It explains costs and credits without assuming that your account has the author's balance or spending rule. Copy examples/costs/worksheet.json into ignored evidence/. Fill current regional rates with matching units and record the source/date. Missing prices intentionally fail.

python3 scripts/estimate-lab-cost.py evidence/my-cost-worksheet.json

Check requests versus millions of requests, duration, retry assumptions, polling, logs and storage. No Free Tier or credit is assumed. Include AI provider charges separately.

Set an alert and a response

Inspect existing AWS Budgets before adding an account-wide monthly cost budget. Use a recipient you control and verify saved thresholds and delivery when feasible. Define who responds. Budget actions have specific capabilities and can disrupt workloads; they are not universal cleanup switches.

Limit the experiment

The A02/A03 source restricts concurrency, timeout, queue retries and data size, requires an estimate and working budget, and makes handlers refuse work after expiry. Storage and queue polling can still cost money until resources are removed. Destroy the isolated stack and verify exact resources are absent. Check delayed billing later.

Use AI as your engineering partner

Ask Codex: “Read this plan and dated worksheet. Calculate with code, list missing charges, inspect units and identify retained resources. Do not deploy.” Verify inputs as well as arithmetic. Challenge it with an unplanned NAT gateway or an endless retry loop. Explain why its estimate is not a guarantee.

Independent challenge

Design a response to a surprise spending alert without deleting shared data. Separate detection, limiting new work, safe teardown and later billing reconciliation. Record what the controls cannot enforce.

Evidence to collect

  • A dated price/usage worksheet with no unexplained omissions
  • A working budget and explicit run/stop limits
  • A cleanup inventory and later billing reconciliation, separately labeled
  • An explanation of why alerts and expiry are not hard dollar caps

Use the source bundle’s examples/advanced/evidence-template.json. Keep credentials and private data out of shared results.
