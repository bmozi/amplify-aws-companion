# SOA-C03 Domain 5 networking skill review

Internal editorial review, October 19, 2026, against [official CloudOps Domain 5](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/sysops-administrator-associate-03/sysops-administrator-associate-03-domain5.html). All twelve skills are mapped **partial** to [Unit 05's whole-request lesson](../../reader/BOOK-LOCATORS.md#book-4448eae645ea531e), its [evidence-boundary figure](../../book/figures/network-evidence-boundaries.svg), [eleven changed-condition cases](../../examples/network-evidence-path/README.md), [assessor](../../examples/network-evidence-path/ASSESSOR.md), and relevant existing local labs. A design case or a local route model does not implement an AWS VPC, DNS policy, edge distribution or hybrid connection.

The Route 53 [public query log](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/Route53/latest/DeveloperGuide/query-logs.html) cannot observe resolver-cache answers. A [VPC Flow Log record](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/vpc/latest/userguide/flow-log-records.html) aggregates interface traffic and may arrive late or skip data; `ACCEPT` is not an application receipt. [CloudFront caching](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonCloudFront/latest/DeveloperGuide/ConfiguringCaching.html) needs a workload-specific key/TTL policy. The cases and local labs make no AWS calls. No independent learner review, live network/cost result or complete exam coverage is claimed.
