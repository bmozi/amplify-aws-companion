Internal editorial review, October 19, 2026, against the current [official CloudOps task 1.1 guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/sysops-administrator-associate-03/sysops-administrator-associate-03-domain1.html#sysops-administrator-associate-03-domain1-task1). All five skill points have **partial** source, explanation and changed-case evidence. The [Unit 08 log-to-operator lesson](../../reader/BOOK-LOCATORS.md#book-d418ac7999cb8497), [diagram](../../book/figures/cloudwatch-alert-evidence-path.svg), [Terraform/agent source and six-case packet](../../examples/cloudwatch-alert-path/README.md), and [separate assessor](../../examples/cloudwatch-alert-path/ASSESSOR.md) are the basis. `terraform fmt -check` and `terraform validate` passed locally against HashiCorp AWS provider 6.69.1; this is not cloud configuration or a learner performance result.

| 1.1.1 Configure monitoring and logging for workloads | Terraform declares a Standard log group, filter, metric alarm and dashboard; chapter distinguishes workload logs, metrics and CloudTrail audit. The example defaults to no resources and has not been planned or applied against AWS. Further certification coverage, independent technical review and learner testing remain open.
