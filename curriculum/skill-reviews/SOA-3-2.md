[Systems Manager Automation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/systems-manager/latest/userguide/systems-manager-automation.html) provides runbooks and rate controls, with current charges to estimate rather than assuming a new-account free tier. [S3 Event Notifications](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/EventNotifications.html) are designed for at-least-once delivery and can self-trigger if output matches the input notification. [EventBridge](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/eventbridge/latest/userguide/eb-what-is.html) can route events; routing is not proof of effect. [AWS DevOps Agent](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/devopsagent/latest/userguide/production-operations-proactive-incident-prevention.html) can propose improvements, which remain subject to resource-owner review and verification. These are service capabilities, not evidence they were enabled in an account. Organization-wide operations remain design-only; no independent learner or live AWS result is claimed.
