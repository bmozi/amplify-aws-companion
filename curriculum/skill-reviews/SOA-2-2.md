Internal editorial review, October 19, 2026, against the [official CloudOps Domain 2 task 2.2](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/sysops-administrator-associate-03/sysops-administrator-associate-03-domain2.html#sysops-administrator-associate-03-domain2-task2). Both numbered skills now have individual **partial** links to the [Unit 07 lesson](../../reader/BOOK-LOCATORS.md#book-caa0fef97a6b2759), [figure](../../book/figures/failover-health-to-customer.svg), [six changed cases](../../examples/failover-health-path/README.md), [assessor](../../examples/failover-health-path/ASSESSOR.md), local decision gate and [E05](../EXERCISES.md#e05). No selected AWS service was configured or failed over.

| Official skill | Teaching and local assessment evidence | Still required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.2.1 Configure and troubleshoot ELB and Route 53 health checks | The lesson separates DNS record choice, cached clients, ALB target health and customer checks. Cases 1–4 change primary/secondary and target states. The local model catches Route 53's checked-pair last-resort primary answer and ALB's registered all-unhealthy fail-open case. Earlier network and observation units discuss paths, matcher, thresholds and reason codes. | A selected public/private record hierarchy and alias/health configuration, actual target status and reason codes, safe fault test, observed resolver/client behavior and customer result, effective authority, current costs, cleanup and independent learner review. |
| 2.2.2 Configure fault-tolerant systems such as Multi-AZ | Unit 07 identifies separate AZ app capacity, a service-managed RDS Multi-AZ DB instance standby, writer authority, in-flight uncertainty and customer evidence. Cases 5–6 show that 55 useful bookings/minute cannot meet 80 arrivals and that green health/failover status does not prove the old/new booking or tenant denial. Earlier local two-worker and reliability-growth exercises test bounded pieces. | Selected deployment in supported AZs with complete dependencies, actual failure/reconnect and safe admission, measured surviving throughput and RTO/RPO, data correctness and denied access, current full cost, cleanup and independent learner review. |

The local model deliberately covers only a pair of failover records with checks on both and a registered ALB target group. It rejects an empty target group instead of inventing that service case. The “reopen all clients” result is a conservative fictional gate, not a real Route 53, ELB, RDS, DNS or customer probe. Source boundaries were checked against [Route 53 record selection](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/Route53/latest/DeveloperGuide/health-checks-how-route-53-chooses-records.html), [ALB target health](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/elasticloadbalancing/latest/application/target-group-health-checks.html) and [RDS Multi-AZ DB instances](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonRDS/latest/UserGuide/Concepts.MultiAZSingleStandby.html). No current price, credit balance, independent technical review, learner result or complete certification coverage is claimed.
