# SOA-C03 task 2.1 elastic-capacity skill review

Internal editorial review, October 19, 2026, against the [official CloudOps Domain 2 task 2.1](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/sysops-administrator-associate-03/sysops-administrator-associate-03-domain2.html#sysops-administrator-associate-03-domain2-task1). Its three numbered skills are individually mapped **partially** to the [illustrated Unit 02 lesson](../../reader/BOOK-LOCATORS.md#book-d1b4552f4da8e9ca), [three-dial figure](../../book/figures/elastic-capacity-three-dials.svg), [seven changed-condition cases](../../examples/elastic-capacity-path/README.md), [separate assessor](../../examples/elastic-capacity-path/ASSESSOR.md), local arithmetic model and [E07](../EXERCISES.md#e07). These are authored and local evidence, not AWS implementation or learner validation.

| Official skill | Current teaching and assessment evidence | Still required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.1.1 Configure and manage compute scaling | Unit 02 distinguishes desired from healthy ready workers, per-worker signal, warmup, min/max, downstream limit and safe scale-in. | 2.1.3 Configure and manage managed-database scaling | Unit 02 separates RDS storage/read/primary-write concerns from DynamoDB table/index read/write and throttling reasons. Cases 5–7 change service, pressure source and write mix; local arithmetic catches the new binding limit. | Selected engine/mode and supported Region, real query or throttle evidence, scoped configuration, safe change/rollback, consistency/recovery and measured customer behavior, complete cost, cleanup, learner review. |

The local model rejects claiming writes as shared cache hits and reports zero ready workers as an app ceiling. It assumes independent steady ceilings and cannot model p95/p99, burst credits, queueing, cache eviction, hot partitions, scaling delay or service behavior. Official service guidance used for the boundaries: [EC2 target tracking](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/autoscaling/ec2/userguide/as-scaling-target-tracking.html), [CloudFront cache key](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonCloudFront/latest/DeveloperGuide/understanding-the-cache-key.html), [RDS storage autoscaling](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonRDS/latest/UserGuide/USER_PIOPS.Autoscaling.html), [DynamoDB provisioned auto scaling](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazondynamodb/latest/developerguide/AutoScaling.html) and [throttling diagnosis](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazondynamodb/latest/developerguide/troubleshooting-throttling-diagnostics.html). Current prices and exact selected-service configuration remain unverified. Organization-dependent work remains design-only in the personal Free-plan account.
