On October 29, 2026, the five AWS SOA-C03 task lists were compared with the identifiers in [coverage.csv](coverage.csv) and the [current chapter entry points](CURRENT-CERTIFICATION-TASK-ENTRYPOINTS.md). The earlier labels for 3.2 and 5.1 were broadened to include event-driven operations and network optimization. The labels for 4.1 and 4.2 were also broadened: AWS includes compliance monitoring and security tools under the former, and infrastructure protection and finding remediation as well as data protection under the latter. These are short navigation labels, not official task quotations or a complete task description. Readers should consult the linked guide and individual skills before deciding what to study.

The structural curriculum checker passes. This review compared task identifiers and selected label scope; it did **not** independently evaluate every mapped chapter, compare all individual skills against current services, test AWS implementations, or observe learners. The CloudOps maps remain partial and require refresh before release. No AWS account, resource or credit was used.
