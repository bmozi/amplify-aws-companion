# Professional architecture domain 1: study map

Use the [AWS domain 1 guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-professional-03/solutions-architect-professional-03-domain1.html) with these lesson and exercise links. **Partial** means the material practices part of a skill; the last column identifies evidence or practice still needed. It does not establish mastery or exam readiness. Check the current guide for your scheduled exam and verify AWS-specific claims before a real implementation.
