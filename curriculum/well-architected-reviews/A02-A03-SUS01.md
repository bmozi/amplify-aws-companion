# SUS 01: a Region is a business and evidence decision

Reviewed October 19, 2026 against the single current [AWS Well-Architected SUS 01 practice](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/w2aac19c17b7b5.html): [choose a Region using business requirements and sustainability goals](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/sus_sus_region_a2.html). This is a teaching and design review of A02 repair processing and A03 reservations, not a Well-Architected Tool result, real Region comparison, legal residency opinion, selected regional price, measured latency/carbon or permission to move the personal lab. The current [A02/A03 runner](../../architectures/reliable-workflows/run.py) and [Terraform](../../architectures/reliable-workflows/infra/main.tf) target the selected Ohio lab configuration; a new Region would need a separate, cost-approved plan and complete customer-path test.

## Maya cannot move the last seat with a map pin

Imagine a poster claiming another Region is greener. Maya runs a repair queue and a last-seat reservation. Her customers care that a submitted repair is recoverable and that exactly one person gets the final seat. The poster does not tell her whether a customer record may live in the other location, whether the needed service features are available, whether the seat decision crosses a slower network, or how many additional copies, requests and transfers a split design creates.

![A fictional Region choice first rejects violated customer and service requirements, keeps missing latency and whole-run cost evidence pending, then compares only compatible sustainability observations. Passing the gates yields a paper shortlist, not a cloud deployment.](../../book/figures/region-choice-gates.svg)

AWS's SUS01-BP01 orders the thinking: shortlist Regions against compliance, required services/features, cost and user latency; then apply a sustainability method consistent with the organization's goals. Region availability, prices, network paths and carbon accounting can change. A lower published grid or vendor-level number is a **candidate input**, not proof that this workload's full-path impact falls after the move. If data stays in Ohio while a frontend moves, that is a split architecture with new transfer, consistency, authority and recovery questions—not the same workload in a different place.

The [local fictional shortlist](../../examples/region-selection/README.md) has three invented labels. `region-alpha` passes declared paper inputs. `region-beta` offers a lower toy carbon number but fails customer residency, so it is rejected. `region-gamma` has no transfer/replication cost coverage, so it stays pending. Even two paper candidates with matching carbon method, workload, boundary and period can only have their supplied toy numbers ordered; the exercise has no measured emissions or actual Region decision. Its USD 5 estimated-run and USD 100 projected-reserve checks are **fictional case inputs**. They do not know today's AWS Free-plan eligibility, account credits or balance.

| Decision layer | What the A02/A03 owner must establish | What this project currently has |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer and legal location | Allowed data/process locations, identity and recovery requirements, owner approval. | No production customer residency policy; synthetic lab records only. |
| Feasible service path | Required exact service/feature availability, synchronous seat authority, repair receipt/retry path, cross-Region dependencies. | One selected Ohio Terraform design; no alternative Region deployment or feature test. |
| Experience and recovery | Representative user latency, queue age, last-seat correctness, regional outage/recovery behavior. | Local/emulated behavior and paper cases, not a cross-Region or customer benchmark. |
| Whole-run cost and stewardship | Current regional service rates, requests, transfer, replication, logs, backups, cleanup, plan/credit eligibility, and spending and reserve limits for the account in use. | A conservative guarded lab policy; no approved alternate-Region whole-run estimate or current account credit query. |
| Sustainability comparison | Approved method, workload denominator, same time period and boundary, data movement, measured resource and carbon evidence. | Fictional proxy exercises only; no Region-specific environmental result. |

## Changed-condition exercise: the lower number fails the customer

A colleague proposes `region-beta` because its **invented** carbon score is 50 versus `region-alpha`'s 80. Both scores are arbitrary classroom units. The contract disallows customer records in beta. A second proposal puts only the browser frontend in beta while keeping the last-seat table and repair queue in alpha. A third uses `region-gamma`, where service checks pass but cross-Region transfer and replica costs have not been estimated. Run the [local shortlist](../../examples/region-selection/README.md) before reading the [assessor](A02-A03-SUS01-ASSESSOR.md).

Decide which option is rejected, pending or a paper candidate. For the split frontend, draw the request, token, authoritative write and response path; list the new data movement, customer latency, authorization and recovery questions. **Change:** the business later approves beta for a non-sensitive public guide only. Does that approval cover private repair records or last-seat transactions? **Change again:** a compatible carbon number uses a different accounting method and a different month. Can the numbers be ranked as if they describe the same workload? Ask Codex to propose the most convincing Region move, then require it to identify one hard constraint, one unknown cost meter and one measurement-boundary mismatch that could defeat its own recommendation. Verify current AWS service and price assertions before any real plan.

**Review disposition:** the single SUS 01 practice now has a workload-specific lesson, illustrated gates, a local negative-control shortlist and an authored changed-case assessor. The current personal lab remains in Ohio. No alternate Region, current account balance/eligibility, actual regional price, latency, service feature, legal policy, measured energy/carbon, independent learner response or final-book visual review was obtained. No AWS resource or credit was used.
