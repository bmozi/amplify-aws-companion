# SEC 03: who can wear the badge, and which doors open?

Reviewed October 19, 2026 against AWS's [nine SEC 03 practices](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/sec-03.html). This is an authored teaching review of A01's website publisher and A02/A03 application roles, not a Well-Architected Tool assessment or an effective-permission audit. The [identity lesson](../../reader/BOOK-LOCATORS.md#book-47726780fac09ca7), [publisher Terraform candidate](../../infra/github-publisher/main.tf), [read-only account preflight](../../scripts/publisher-account-preflight.py), and [synthetic document-comparison lab](../../examples/publisher-role-evidence/README.md) are the local evidence base. Current AWS account role state has not been observed in this packet.

## The one-document trap

Maya reviews an inline policy that names only the `lab` branch. She concludes that the publisher cannot touch another branch. That conclusion is too large for the evidence: another policy may be attached, a resource may grant access through a different path, or the role in AWS may not match her local file. The local lab compares a declared recipe with a synthetic *read-only receipt*; one altered attachment or unreadable inventory changes the review state. Even a matching receipt is only a document comparison, not a successful AWS session or customer-visible publication.

Do not call routine root publishing an emergency process. An enterprise multi-account break-glass design stays on paper here. |
| [BP04 — Reduce permissions continuously](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/sec_permissions_continuous_reduction.html) | The policy-source checker and new account receipt offer a repeatable comparison; earlier standalone simulation covered an older policy version. | Review actual usage and attachments over time, decide which grants remain necessary, then re-test intended and forbidden work. | [BP05 — Define guardrails for your organization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/sec_permissions_define_guardrails.html) | The local helper rejects root for deployment; Unit 06 teaches boundaries and SCP limitations. | A local script is not an IAM guardrail. Organization-level SCP/Control Tower work is design-only in this review; do not create an Organization to fill a checkbox. |
| [BP06 — Manage access by lifecycle](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/sec_permissions_lifecycle.html) | Unit 06's workforce path distinguishes joining, changing jobs and leaving; OIDC sessions are short-lived by design. | Verify real identity ownership, role review on repo/maintainer changes, active sessions and retirement of the publisher route. | [BP07 — Analyze public and cross-account access](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/sec_permissions_analyze_cross_account.html) | The account receipt inventories an OIDC provider and role trust; the permission-review exercise warns that resource-policy grants can change the result. | Review actual resource-based policies and external access findings, including exact principal and account context. An OIDC trust policy is not proof that no public resource exists. |
| [BP08 — Share securely within the organization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/sec_permissions_share_securely.html) | Unit 16 and multi-account design cases discuss ownership and separate access paths. | No AWS Organization or member-account share is configured for this trial. Treat this as a future business design exercise, not an implemented control. |

For a separate supplier data share, define the supplier principal, data boundary, expiry, audit and revocation; the publisher pattern cannot be copied as a data-sharing permission. |

## Changed-condition exercise: a green file and a wider role

Complete the [three-receipt local lab](../../examples/publisher-role-evidence/README.md) before its assessor. Then suppose the role documents matched at 10:00, an administrator attached a new managed policy at 10:05, and a GitHub job starts at 10:10. Which receipt is stale? What can be said about the new policy before reading its default version? What safe evidence is needed to decide whether an unrelated app is reachable? Ask Codex to trace identity, trust, grant, restriction and downstream service role; have it identify which statement comes from source versus account observation. Compare with the [separate assessor](A01-A03-SEC03-ASSESSOR.md).

**Disposition:** all nine practices now have workload-specific teaching decisions and explicit unimplemented boundaries. The synthetic checker and mocked account-preflight tests establish local behavior only. Actual role, policy version, effective permission, emergency access, organizational sharing, service execution, customer denial, and independent learner evidence remain open.
