## One situation, five different questions

The two-hour repair-request experiment has ended. Its Lambda handler refuses new work after its expiry. Can Maya tell the next learner that the lab costs nothing now? No. The table, queues, functions, event-source mapping, log groups and roles may still exist. The mapping may keep polling. Stored records and logs may remain. Credits can make a net bill look small while resources are still consuming billable units. Follow five questions in order:

```text
Which resources belong to the run?  →  Who may remove them and when?
             ↓                                  ↓
What was actually removed?         ←  What survived or failed removal?
             ↓
Which data must remain, for how long, and who pays for it?
```

The arrows describe decisions and evidence handoffs, not an automated AWS workflow. A resource tag helps identify an owner; it does not stop a meter. An expired handler stops one application path; it does not destroy Terraform resources. A Terraform destroy result helps prove removal of managed resources; it does not by itself cover separately created resources, retained data or delayed billing.

## Changed-condition exercise: a late invoice

At 14:00 the handler expires. At 14:05 Terraform destroy reports success. At 14:10 the account's net charge is still zero. At 14:20 an independent inventory finds one log group created by an earlier manual experiment and a queue in another Region with a similar name. At the next billing refresh, pre-credit usage increases. Which claims can the operator make? What must be checked before deleting either extra resource? Write a four-line receipt: **application work**, **exact managed inventory**, **extra-resource ownership**, and **billing period/measure**. Then change the story: a reviewer has requested a seven-day copy of one synthetic test record. Specify a retained-resource owner, expiry and price review without keeping the whole table by habit. Ask Codex to challenge each claim with a missing observation, then inspect the actual source, inventory and bill yourself. Compare your answer with the [separate assessor](A02-A03-COST04-ASSESSOR.md) afterward.

**Review disposition:** all five practices now have workload-specific questions and artifact pointers. BP04 is an explicit design tradeoff; BP01–03 and BP05 have local planning evidence only. No live provider behavior, post-run invoice, independent learner answer, or exact-final book review was produced. This review advances the best-practice-level *teaching and design* gap for COST 04; it does not close implementation compliance or the wider Well-Architected workload review.
