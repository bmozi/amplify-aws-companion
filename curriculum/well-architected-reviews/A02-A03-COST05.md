# COST 05: compare the service, not one price tag

Reviewed October 19, 2026 against AWS's [six COST 05 practices](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/cost-05.html). This is a **workload-specific teaching review** of A02 repair requests and A03 last-seat reservations, not a Well-Architected Tool assessment or a live service benchmark. The [fictional cost lab](../../examples/costs/service-selection/README.md) supplies exact, deliberately invented arithmetic. The [A02/A03 source](../../architectures/reliable-workflows/infra/main.tf) and [bounded-run packet](../../examples/costs/A02-A03-BOUNDED-RUN.md) supply architecture and meter questions. Check the exact current plan before a cloud run.

## One cheap line, an expensive decision

Maya sees a low per-job price and wants to replace a managed worker with a permanently running fleet. The self-operated design really is cheaper in the lab's busy months. Over the entire specified year, setup-period fixed cost, labor and license reverse that result. Even that full-year arithmetic is conditional: both candidates must first prove the same five-minute deadline, durable acceptance and one-hour recovery. For A03, a confirmed last seat cannot be silently redefined as an eventually completed queue job. The price comparison follows the customer promise; it cannot rewrite it.

| AWS practice | A02/A03 application and local evidence | Missing proof or decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [BP01 — Align with organization requirements](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/cost_select_service_requirements.html) | The case fixes acceptance, deadline and recovery conditions. A02 can queue durable repair work; A03's immediate confirmation needs a different correctness boundary. | Obtain actual business deadline, data retention, location, security and staffing requirements. A service that fails a hard requirement is not a cheaper equivalent. |
| [BP02 — Analyze all workload components](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/cost_select_service_analyze_all.html) | The lab lists fixed, usage, storage, transfer, logs, operations and license amounts, and changes demand across two phases. The A02/A03 run packet adds queue polling and retained-resource questions. | Identify every real request, idle, retry, storage, backup, network and support meter for the selected services and Region. The fictional fixture is intentionally incomplete for a live purchase. |
| [BP03 — Analyze thorough ownership cost](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/cost_select_service_thorough_analysis.html) | The assessor compares twelve-month totals, not only the growth month's per-job line, and assigns a visible labor rate. | Price migration, patching, on-call, testing, incident work, support and exit cost for the actual team. Measure operations hours rather than inheriting the exercise's assumptions. |
| [BP04 — Consider licensing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/cost_select_service_licensing.html) | The self-operated candidate exposes a license line so it cannot disappear in an instance comparison. | Read the exact license, scaling, support, recovery-copy and portability terms. Zero in a toy input is not proof of no obligation. |
| [BP05 — Select cost-effective components](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/cost_select_service_select_for_cost.html) | Managed queue/worker/data and self-operated workers/database are bounded candidates; the case shows a cost preference can change with volume. | Test the full path under peak/failure conditions and select only among candidates that meet the promise. Event-driven does not mean zero idle or polling cost in the A02 SQS/Lambda design. |
| [BP06 — Analyze costs over time](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/cost_select_service_analyze_over_time.html) | The pilot and growth phases reverse the monthly winner, while the twelve-month total favors the other candidate. | Revisit with observed volumes, price/terms changes, performance and failure results. Keep a dated decision record and change threshold; do not extrapolate a single month forever. |

## Changed-condition exercise

Complete the [lab](../../examples/costs/service-selection/README.md) before reading its assessor. Then change growth from 500,000 to 1,000,000 jobs per month and imagine the self-operated database misses its one-hour recovery objective during a zone loss. State which cost conclusion changed, which candidate remains ineligible, and which new evidence would let the team reconsider. Have Codex challenge the missing network and labor assumptions. Check its numbers with the local script; check its service claims with current AWS documentation and a bounded test plan. The [separate assessor](A02-A03-COST05-ASSESSOR.md) distinguishes cost arithmetic from architecture acceptance.

**Disposition:** all six practices now have workload-specific teaching questions, source pointers and an assessable changed case. This closes only the COST 05 *question-mapping-to-teaching* gap. Actual price collection, eligible-service comparison, implementation, workload measurement, license review, billing reconciliation and independent learner assessment remain open. No AWS call or credit was used.
