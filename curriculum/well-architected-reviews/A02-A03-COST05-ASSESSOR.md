# Assessor: COST 05 changed demand and failed recovery

Read after attempting the [learner case](A02-A03-COST05.md#changed-condition-exercise). This is author reasoning over a fictional case, not a scored response.

At 1,000,000 growth jobs per month, the script calculates **$1,650 managed** and **$988 self-operated** per month. The growth-month cost ranking now favors self-operation by $662 under the invented inputs. With the same two pilot months and ten changed growth months, the managed twelve-month total is $16,803 and the self-operated total is $11,256.60. These figures exclude migration and other unmodeled obligations. They establish only arithmetic under this fixture.

The self-operated candidate's missed one-hour recovery objective makes it **ineligible under the stated customer promise**, regardless of its lower modeled cost. A learner should not simply select it, silently loosen the recovery objective, or label the managed design proven. Require an actual failure drill or a reviewed design change, a measured recovery time, data-loss observation, peak deadline/correctness result, complete current regional estimate and license review. Then revisit the total cost over the remaining expected lifetime.

A strong answer names at least one omitted meter, such as queue polling, backup/recovery copy, cross-zone or internet transfer, support or incident work, and says whether the candidate actually crosses that billing boundary. It challenges the assumed two versus eight monthly operations hours. It separates a feasible option, a priced option, a deployed option and a measured customer outcome. No independent learner result exists.
