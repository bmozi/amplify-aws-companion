# Choose the right professional exam guide

Before making a study plan, check the exam code and appointment date on your registration. AWS updates certifications, and two versions can use different domain names and objectives. The [AWS comparison](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-professional-03/sap-03-comparison.html) describes the SAP-C02 and SAP-C03 transition; the [professional certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-solutions-architect-professional/) and your booking confirmation are the places to check current availability.

AWS's comparison consulted for this guide listed SAP-C02 through November 16, 2026 and SAP-C03 beginning November 17, 2026. Verify these dates and your scheduled version with AWS rather than relying on a printed date or assuming an appointment is available.

Use the [SAP-C03 learning map](../curriculum/SAP-C03-GAP-QUEUE.md) for the five domains, lessons and exercises in this companion. If your appointment is for SAP-C02, follow its [official exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-professional-02/solutions-architect-professional-02.html) and compare its objectives with your study plan. Do not assume that a lesson linked to one version covers every objective in another.

A practice question measures a decision under the facts given. To build professional skill, explain your choice, challenge it with a changed requirement and identify what a real implementation would need to prove. A mapping or a correct answer does not guarantee an exam result.
