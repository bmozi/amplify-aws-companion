## Interpret partial evidence correctly

The inherited ledger combines missing live behavior, measured cost, independent review and learner performance in `remaining_work`. Those do not all mean the prose is absent. Conversely, an existing section heading and test cannot establish that a skill is fully taught. Maintain separate instruction, assessment, technical verification and learner evidence. Do not relabel all 189 rows complete after a link check, a local suite, or John's eventual pass.
