# Task 3.5 skill practice

Attempt these cases before consulting assessor material. Record your decision, rejected alternative, changed-case reasoning and uncertainty. Note any assistance or prior exposure.

## Skill 02

Give independent consumers recoverable progress

A sales source feeds an operational dashboard and a fraud application. The dashboard needs fresh results; fraud must recompute the preceding seven days after a rule repair. Deliveries can repeat or arrive late, and either consumer may be unavailable independently. Compare a shared work queue with independently progressing consumers over retained history. Draw event identity, each consumer's progress, access boundaries, retained source and output version. Trace a crash before the effect and one after the effect but before progress. Then start a long replay near the retention limit. Explain the risk, the margin or alternative source needed, and why rebuilding analysis differs from resending customer receipts.

## Skill 07

Preserve meaning while changing representation

A CSV-to-Parquet conversion must preserve the identifier `001`, distinguish literal `NULL` from a missing value, handle refunds and require a timezone marker. Before running the conversion exercise, specify accepted and rejected inputs and an independent comparison of complete rows. Explain how a defect could preserve the total and row count while changing identity. Now introduce a second currency, explicit timestamp offsets, a missing historical channel and an unknown schema version. State compatibility decisions, expected results, unknown facts and publication/retention rules without inventing historical data. Compare whole-batch rejection with durable quarantine and identify concurrency and crash-recovery limits.
