# SAP-C02 task 4.1 migration assessment review

Internal editorial review, October 19, 2026, against the transitional [official task 4.1 guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-professional-02/solutions-architect-professional-02-domain4.html#solutions-architect-professional-02-domain4-task1). Four knowledge and three skill points have **partial** authored teaching and an original changed-case packet. These are not proof of exam readiness, a real application assessment or future SAP-C03 coverage.

| Point | Teaching and assessment evidence | Still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge 1: assessment and tracking tools | Unit 12 separates progress tracking from owner acceptance; packet asks which source a tracker ingests and who maintains it. | Selected tool integration, permission and freshness evidence. |
| Knowledge 2: portfolio assessment | Four workloads have different business promises, dependency confidence and cost data; change card invalidates earlier assumptions. | Real owner interviews, measured inventory and utilization. |
| Knowledge 3: asset planning | Unit lists source components, data, licenses, support and recovery; packet includes a physical controller, shared identity and retained archive. | Verified assets, license/support terms and selected target. |
| Knowledge 4: prioritization and waves | Unit gates waves on dependency groups, capacity and windows; packet limits customer-facing rehearsals and changes a quiet-window assumption. | Approved schedule, capacity and observed rehearsal. |
| Skill 1: complete assessment | Reader must label observed, confirmed and unknown facts for each workload. | Independent learner response and real assessment. |
| Skill 2: evaluate 7Rs | Reader must choose per workload, including retention and a conditional retirement; assessor accepts reasoned alternatives. | Vendor and selected-platform compatibility evidence. |
| Skill 3: evaluate TCO | Three 12-month comparisons are arithmetic only; shared identity and omitted charges prevent a savings claim. | Full priced scope and finance review. |

The [packet](../../examples/migration-portfolio/README.md), [assessor](../../examples/migration-portfolio/ASSESSOR.md) and [figure](../../book/figures/migration-portfolio-gates.svg) are local teaching material.
