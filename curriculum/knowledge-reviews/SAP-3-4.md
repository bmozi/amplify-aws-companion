# SAP-C02 task 3.4 reliability-improvement review

Internal editorial review, October 19, 2026, against the transitional [official task 3.4 guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-professional-02/solutions-architect-professional-02-domain3.html#solutions-architect-professional-02-domain3-task4). Six knowledge points and four skills have **partial** teaching and changed-case evidence. This does not establish a deployed multi-AZ or multi-Region system, certification readiness or SAP-C03 coverage.

| Point | Teaching and assessment evidence | Still needed |
